# Schoolido Twist content

The Twist primary curriculum (content) and the research behind it, in one
git repo kept separate from schoolido-learning.

## Layout

- **twist/**: the content. Curricula (de-DE, en-GB) on one shared skill
  library, the lessons, the asset bank, docs and generated views.
  twist/AGENTS.md adds its own rules; twist/PLAN.md holds the steps.
  - Lessons live where the curriculum file places them:
    `twist/lessons/<curriculum>/<year>/<subject>/<skill>.yaml`; a track's
    set (DaZ) in `twist/lessons/<curriculum>/<year>/<track>/<skill>.yaml`
    (twist/docs/model.md, Tracks). The build refuses a file in any other
    folder.
  - views/ is generated by `node twist/build.mjs`: course-de-DE.md (the
    course as authored), tracking.md (coverage, cost, review passes, open
    points; its Reviews section comes from twist/reviews.yaml, so add a pass
    there after each review), coverage and the library.
  - **pages/**: pages built from the content.
    - site/: how the content works and fits together: the chain, curricula,
      task types, components, quality and R&D. Built from the records by
      `node twist/pages/site/build.mjs`; never edit index.html by hand.
    - player/: Twist Preview, the app in a phone frame (Start, Study,
      Practice and Me tabs): the lessons with pictures, drawn components
      and narration, and practice
      (twist/docs/practice.md): sessions built by player/practice-rules.mjs
      (tests: `cd twist && npm test`) for a pretend child, with drill banks
      from twist/drills/; its screens are player/practice-ui.js and
      player/practice.css, and the tabs around them player/app-ui.js and
      player/app.css. Study opens on the track of the app (stones,
      arcs, islands); player/study/ holds that art, copied from
      ist-schoolido's assets/images/study (french.svg is drawn here in the
      same style); player/tabs/ holds the app's tab icons
      (assets/images/tabs); Home, Study and Practice are drawn in
      app-ui.js (TAB_ART) instead, so each plays once when its tab is
      opened and the tab pill slides over. The app's animations (Rive and
      Lottie) and sound effects are in player/anim/, copied from
      ist-schoolido's develop (assets/animations, assets/images/lotties,
      assets/audio) and played by player/anim-ui.js where the app plays
      them: splash, lesson loading, the Study skate, the celebrations
      after a lesson. Animated badges: one folder each in
      assets/badges/<Name>/ (the badge's English name; an SVG that plays its
      intro and loops, and the WAV that starts with it), found by the build;
      `node twist/assets/tools/badge-stills.cjs` (needs Playwright) makes
      their stills in assets/badges/stills/ for the small places. The rest
      stay as drawn in assets/badges. assets/icons/ holds Twist's own icons
      (the streak's flame, stars, badges), each still and animated. The Parents Lab tab shows pages/parents-lab, the ideas for the
      parents dashboard (player/parents-ui.js, parents.css).
      The right panel follows the open screen (player/side-ui.js): each
      tab and the lesson player get their own controls; a lesson picked in
      the left list plays in the phone.
      Every drawing is in player/drawn.js, which the
      build inlines and the app can use as it is. `node twist/pages/player/build.mjs`; it
      plays audio from twist/assets/narration/<voice>/, which is in git
      (about 3 GB); after a content change, narrate the new lines with
      twist/assets/tools/narration.py and commit the new clips.
    - drawings/: every drawn component with a real example and its
      picture, the player's Drawings tab; `node twist/pages/drawings/build.cjs`
      (needs Playwright) writes index.html, standalone.html (drawn.js on its
      own) and drawn/; never edit them by hand.
    - types/: every task type with a screen; `node twist/pages/types/shots.cjs`
      (needs Playwright) retakes the shots. Keep it in step with
      twist/docs/task-types.md. A copy is published as a claude.ai artifact.
    - rewards/: stars and badges, built from twist/docs/rewards.md by
      `node twist/pages/rewards/build.mjs`; never edit index.html by hand.
    - sources/: where every text, picture and sound on a lesson screen comes
      from (the player's label, the v2 field, the record key or asset file,
      the S3 key), written by hand; a copy is published as a claude.ai
      artifact. Update it when a screen, a record key or an asset path
      changes.
    - review/: every lesson of a curriculum as printable pages for a
      teacher review, one PDF per year and subject plus one combined:
      `node twist/pages/review/build.mjs [de-DE]` (needs Google Chrome and
      pdfunite); output in review/out/, not in git.
    - parents-lab/: the Parents Lab, ideas for the parents dashboard
      (the player's Parents Lab tab), one plate per
      component with the parent's question it answers and the data it
      needs, drawn with a pretend child; written by hand.
    - handoff/: the handoff to the backend, mobile app and parent dashboard
      teams (IST-B2C/backend, ist-schoolido, IST-B2C/Parent-dashboard),
      written by hand; a copy is published as a claude.ai artifact. Update it
      when twist/ changes what those teams build.
- **rnd/**: the research: the captured sources (anton/, official-de/,
  official-gb/), the catalogue, crosswalk, research site, readiness review
  and the research write-ups. rnd/AGENTS.md has its rules.
- **twist/serve.mjs**: serves pages/ and assets/ (the player with its
  Practice, Drawings and Task types tabs) from a clone to people signed in
  with their own account; twist/server/ holds sign-in, roles, and feedback
  pinned to app screens (twist/docs/feedback.md; the backend's part in
  twist/docs/feedback-backend.md; the player's side is
  pages/player/feedback-ui.js). docker-compose.player.yml runs it in Docker
  (settings in .env.player, accounts in .twist-data/, neither in git), the Makefile wraps it (`make help`), and every push to main deploys
  it to new.dev.twist-learning.com (.github/workflows/deploy-player.yml,
  `make player-deploy`); README.md, "Serve the player on a server"; the host's runbook is
  twist/pages/hosting/index.html (written by hand, published as a claude.ai
  artifact).
- README.md: the quick reference. Keep the root to these, plus config files.

## Rules for every agent session here

- **Keep everything captured.** Never delete, move out, overwrite or "clean
  up" any captured file under rnd/. If disk space or duplicates become a
  problem, stop and ask.
- **Generated outputs are rebuilt, never hand-edited.**
- **Never write into schoolido-learning.** Reading it for comparison is fine.
