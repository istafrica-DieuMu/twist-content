## Method

- Unit of comparison: a Twist lesson (bold title in the catalogue) against an Anton lesson (an entry inside a topic line of the catalogue view). Each matched row pairs one Twist lesson with one Anton lesson; one Twist lesson can have several rows.
- Matching is by what is taught (skill and scope), not by wording. Exact means same skill and scope. Partial means overlapping skill, or different scope or depth; the reason is given in the match cell.
- Grade gap is the absolute difference between the Twist grade and the Klasse (K9/10 counted as 9). Twist lessons that read as German as a foreign language (vocabulary themes, set phrases, culture) are listed under Missing in Anton with the note DaZ-style content, because Anton places that content in DaZ, not Deutsch.
- Anton punctuation was normalised for Markdown: en dashes in topic names became hyphens, German typographic quotes became plain quotes. Twist titles with em dashes were changed the same way. The Twist catalogues are written in English; the German lesson titles are Anton's.
- Anton lessons that carry a Twist match are removed from Missing in Twist. The G1 to G4 Twist catalogues list 103 lessons in total.

## 7. Summary

- Twist has 103 German lessons in G1 to G4. 70 have at least one Anton Deutsch match (20 with an exact match somewhere, 50 partial only). 33 have no match.
- Of the 33 unmatched Twist lessons, 19 are DaZ-style content (vocabulary themes, greetings, set phrases, culture). By grade the unmatched counts are G1 13, G2 9, G3 4, G4 7.
- The matched table has 202 lesson pairs. Most are partial, because Anton lessons are narrow drills and Twist lessons bundle several skills.
- Anton Klasse 1 to 4 has 393 lessons; 241 are matched to a Twist lesson and 152 have no Twist match.
- Unmatched Anton lessons by Klasse: K1 33, K2 32, K3 47, K4 40. The 60 K2 Schreibschrift lessons are matched only partially, to Twist G1 letter formation (Druckschrift versus cursive scripts).
- Biggest unmatched Anton topics in Klasse 1 to 4: K3 Rechtschreibung - Rechtschreibstrategien (9); K1 Sprechen und Zuhören (8); K4 Rechtschreibung - Kurze und lange Selbstlaute (7); K2 Rechtschreibung - Rechtschreibstrategien (6); K4 Rechtschreibung - Rechtschreibstrategien wiederholen (4).
- Anton Klasse 5 and up has 260 lessons (253 unmatched), mostly grammar (Wortarten, Satzglieder, Zeitformen, Unregelmäßige Verben), spelling and literature; Twist's progression stops before all of it.
- Misplaced: 57 rows where Twist teaches the skill earlier than Anton (37 Twist lessons) and 58 rows where Anton teaches it earlier (26 Twist lessons). Largest gap is 5 grades (Twist G4 Opinion Writing and Figurative Language against Anton Klasse 9/10).
- Twist is earlier on cases, Perfekt, question words, adjective detail, subject and predicate, and presentations. Anton is earlier on diphthongs, syllables, plurals, word derivation, umlaut spelling, revising and capitalisation.
- Anton Klasse 1 has no Twist counterpart for Blitzlesen sight-word drills, Erste Sätze lesen, word-level drills (Wortschlangen, Lückenwörter, Wörter abschreiben, Wörter bilden) and most Sprechen und Zuhören lessons (Witze, Zungenbrecher, Geschichten).
- Anton Klasse 2 to 4 grammar and spelling with no Twist lesson: Nomen, Verben and Adjektive kennenlernen, Pronomen, Satzarten and Satzschlusszeichen, Adjektive vergleichen, Futur and Zeitformen, Satzglieder bestimmen, and most Rechtschreibstrategien (Silbenschwingen, Wörter verlängern, Merkwörter).
