## 1. Inputs and method

- Twist side (read only): `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/schoolido-learning/content/de/grade-1/math/module-catalog.md`, and the same path for `grade-2`, `grade-3`, `grade-4`.
- Anton side: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/catalogue/views/de-maths.md` (course ids c-mat-1 to c-mat-9, c-mat-11-algebra, c-mat-11-analysis, c-mat-11-stochastic; exercise counts in brackets).
- Background: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/anatomy.md`; spec: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/crosswalk/README.md`.


- Unit of comparison: a Twist lesson (bold title in the module catalog) against an Anton lesson (entry inside a topic line of de-maths.md). Rows are matched on what is taught and the number range, not on wording.
- Exact means same skill and range. Partial means overlapping skill, a different range or depth, or an Anton title too thin to confirm the skill; the reason is given in the cell.
- Anton lessons are known by title and exercise count only; no lesson content was read, so partial rows may be exact or weaker once content is checked.
- Grade gap is the absolute difference between the Twist grade and the Klasse of the first Anton lesson in the cell. Twist money lessons use US coins and Anton uses euro, so those rows are partial.
- Twist checkpoint modules are assessment-only and are not counted as lessons.
