## Inputs and method

Inputs:

- Twist: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/schoolido-learning/content/en/grade-{1,2,3,4}/math/module-catalog.md` (read only).
- Anton: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/catalogue/views/en_gb-maths.md` (course ids `c-mat-r-gb` to `c-mat-6-gb`, plus four KS3 courses). Exercise counts are in brackets.
- Background and spec: `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/anatomy.md`, `/Users/diemu/ist/b2c/rnd/crosswalk/README.md`.

Method:

- Unit of comparison: a Twist lesson (a bold title in the catalogue, with its parts) against an Anton lesson (an entry inside a topic line).
- Matching is by what is taught (skill and number range), read from Twist part descriptions and inventories and from Anton titles. Anton titles carry no scope text, so a pair whose Anton range or depth cannot be seen is marked partial, with the reason in the match column.
- A currency difference alone (US coins in Twist, UK coins in Anton) does not lower a match.
- The Anton lesson listed first in a row sets the grade gap: the lesson closest in scope, taking the year nearest the aligned year when several fit. Other overlapping Anton lessons follow "Also" and count as covered. Earlier Anton introductions are collected in the Misplaced section.
- Grade gap is Anton year minus aligned year. Negative means Anton teaches it earlier. Positive means Twist teaches it earlier.
- Assessments (Baseline, Checkpoint, Final) are not lessons and are left out. Their nearest Anton analogue is the KS1 and KS2 test practice topics, listed under Missing in Twist.

## Summary

- Twist has 137 Math lessons in G1-G4 (G1 26, G2 32, G3 40, G4 39). 125 match an Anton lesson (68 exact, 57 partial) and 12 have no Anton match.
- Anton Years 1-5 have 534 lessons. 332 are covered by at least one Twist lesson, so 202 have no Twist match. Year 6 has 108 of 116 unmatched, KS3 has 122 (all unmatched), Reception has 20.
- Grade gap: 52 matched rows align (gap 0), 67 have Anton teaching earlier, 6 have Twist teaching earlier.
- Number ranges: Anton teaches each range one year earlier. Addition and subtraction within 10 are in Anton Year 1 against Twist G1, within 100 in Anton Year 2 against Twist G2, within 1,000 in Anton Year 3 against Twist G3. Anton also covers within 20 in Year 1; Twist reaches sums 11-18 only in G2 (Mental Math: make a ten, gap -2).
- Fractions: Twist has no fractions lesson in G1 or G2. Anton teaches halves and quarters in Year 1 and thirds and fractions of shapes and numbers in Year 2. Twist G3 Unit Fractions matches Anton Year 2, a gap of -2, the largest in the file. Like-denominator addition and subtraction appears in Anton Year 3 and Year 4, then again in Year 5, while Twist teaches it once in G4.
- Twist teaches earlier than Anton for Rounding to 10 and 5 by 5 coordinate grids (G2, Anton Year 4), and for Fractions as Division, Adding and Subtracting Decimals, Mean Median and Mode, Interpreting Data and simplifying fractions (G4, Anton Year 6). All are gap +1.
- Largest unmatched Anton topics in Years 1-5: Year 5 Fractions, Decimals and Percentages (16 of 27), Year 2 KS1 Test Practice - Reasoning (12 of 12), Year 4 Fractions and Decimals (10 of 22), Year 2 KS1 Test Practice - Arithmetic (10 of 10), Year 5 Multiplication and Division (9 of 19). Anton Year 1 and 2 lessons on days, months, dates, seasons, left and right, forwards and backwards, KS1 test practice and Block Diagrams have no Twist counterpart.
- Twist lessons with no Anton match (12): all probability lessons (G2 Probability Intro, G3 Probability Concepts, G4 Probability (Developed)), line plots (G3, G4), expanded form (G2 twice, G3), division with remainders and interpreting remainders, subtraction as difference, and 3D shapes with real objects.
- Twist repeats some introductions that Anton teaches once: equal groups and arrays appear in G2 and again in G3 (Anton Year 3 only), and coordinates appear in G2 and G4 (Anton Year 4 only).
- Confidence: 57 of 125 matches are partial, mostly because Anton titles carry no number range or depth. Matches rest on titles and Twist catalogue text, not on exercise content.
