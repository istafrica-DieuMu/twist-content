## Method

Each Twist lesson title was read against every Anton course, topic and lesson title, looking for the same subject matter. Matching is by title and topic only, because the inputs list titles, not lesson content. Every match is therefore "partial" at best. Gap = Twist grade minus Anton Klasse, as an absolute number. Anton's two outdated courses (c-eng-1, c-eng-2) are included and flagged. Anton shows counts in brackets that are exercises, not lessons; the outdated courses list topic items with 0 lessons.

## Which kind of course

**The two are different kinds of course.** Twist de/english is first-language English literacy: phonics, word families, sight words, reading comprehension, paragraph and essay writing, grammar, research and media skills. Only the instruction language is German. The G1 notes say "English for German-speaking children: German instructions, English letters, sounds and words as the target", and G3 mentions where English syllable splitting differs from German, but no lesson teaches themed beginner vocabulary (greetings, colours, family, clothes). Anton Englisch is a foreign-language course: vocabulary themes in Klasse 3 and 4 (and the ungraded Stufe courses), grammar tenses and structures from Klasse 5, then texts and cultural topics. The crosswalk is mostly a gap analysis. The few overlaps are grammar and word-study topics, and Twist teaches them years earlier than Anton.

## Summary

- Verdict: Twist de/english is first-language English literacy taught in German. Anton Englisch is foreign-language English. The overlap is small.
- Matched: 26 of 132 Twist lessons (G1 4, G2 9, G3 5, G4 8) against 43 Anton lessons, in 49 rows. All are partial, none exact, because only titles were compared.
- Missing in Twist: 436 of 475 Anton lessons (non-outdated courses) have no match. The whole of Klasse 3 and 4 vocabulary themes (hello, colours, numbers, family, clothes, food, calendar, weather, hobbies, jobs; c-eng-3 85 lessons, c-eng-4 39 lessons) and both Stufe courses (51 lessons) are absent in Twist.
- Missing in Anton: 106 of 132 Twist lessons (G1 28, G2 32, G3 26, G4 20), mainly phonics, word families, reading comprehension, composition and media skills. Anton has no alphabet, phonics or writing-process lessons.
- Misplaced: 39 pairs differ in grade, all with Twist earlier. Gaps run from 1 (G4 pronouns and conjunctions vs Klasse 5) to 7 (G4 Figurative Language vs Klasse 11), most between 2 and 4.
- The one real overlap in vocabulary is G1 Everyday Vocabulary (body, animals, food, school) against Anton's Body, Animals, Food and School topics. It sorts words, while Anton teaches the English words, so it is partial.
- The biggest content gap for a German-speaking beginner is that Twist has no themed foreign-language vocabulary in G1 to G4, which Anton teaches from Klasse 3 (and Stufe 0 and 1).
- Anton's grammar progression (tenses, passive, conditionals, reported speech, irregular verbs, Klasse 6 to 10) has no Twist counterpart beyond G3 Irregular Verbs and G3 and G4 tense lessons.
- c-eng-1 and c-eng-2 are outdated and have 0 lessons, so they are only topic lists; 4 G1 rows reference c-eng-1 and are flagged.
