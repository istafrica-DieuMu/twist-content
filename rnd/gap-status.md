# Gap status: Anton against Twist, 2026-10-16

The gap comparison in
`anton/notes/prior/2026-10-07-anton-curriculum-research.md` (the Gaps tab
of the research site) compared Anton with the old Schoolido G1 to G4
catalogues. This file checks every gap in it against Twist as it is now.

## Context

Both comparisons are now first language against first language:

| Subject | Anton | Twist | Same context |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | UK English, Reception to Year 6 | en-GB English (lit-en), Reception to Year 6, 163 skills | Yes. It was in the old note too |
| German | Deutsch, Klasse 1 to 4 | de-DE Deutsch (lit-de), Klasse 1 to 4, 174 skills | Yes. In the old note it was not: the old G1 and G2 German was German as a foreign language for English speakers, and G3 and G4 was first-language German |

Twist has no German-as-a-foreign-language track. Its foreign languages are
Englisch in de-DE and French in en-GB, and neither has been compared with
an Anton course.

|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic phonics | Closed in coverage, coarse in grain | Phase 2 and 3 in Reception (`phonics.s-a-t-p` to `ear-air-ure-er`), Phase 5 in Year 1 (`new-graphemes`, `split-digraphs`, `alternative-pronunciations`), `spelling.alternative-spellings` (Year 2).
