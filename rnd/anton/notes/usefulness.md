# Where this feeds Schoolido work

Step 1e note, 2026-10-09. Each line names a finding (ids in
[../../FINDINGS.md](../../FINDINGS.md)), the Schoolido work it could change,
and what to do next. Nothing here has been acted on yet.

## Item types and the app

- **F-001, F-002, F-005 → question-type backlog.** Anton's interaction mix is
  much wider than Twist's (65 trainers, 9 shared types with no Twist
  equivalent). Candidates in order of reach: read-a-passage-then-answer,
  tap-on-picture, written-maths grid, syllable marking (German), timed
  reading. Next: a ranked backlog in schoolido-learning against
  `schemas/question-types/` and what the app renders.
- **F-006 → German literacy.** `syllabifier` (syllable arcs) and `flashRead`
  (Blitzlesen) are standard in German primary teaching and absent from Twist.
  Input for the de/ repair and the German en-LOI standard.

## Lesson flow

- **F-007, F-008 → feedback design.** Retry in place, a reveal after a
  mistake that counts neither right nor wrong, and Prüfen for constructive
  tasks. Compare with Twist's 3-tier hints and repeat-on-failure.
- **F-009 → lesson length.** Anton lessons are 4 to 8 exercises of about 14
  items. A Twist part is one 6 to 7 step sitting of 25 to 35 questions.
  Input for the depth contract.
- **F-010 → motivation.** Stars per exercise, trophies for tests, coins for
  games. Twist has none of these in content; this is an app decision.

## Curriculum and catalogues
