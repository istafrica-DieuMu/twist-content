# Ethik

Priority 5. Taught instead of Religion; the name differs by Land (NRW:
Praktische Philosophie). Twist does Ethik only: it fits every family, while
Religion is split by denomination.

Not every Land teaches it in Grundschule (KMK report on Ethik, as amended
2020). From Klasse 1: Bayern, Sachsen, Sachsen-Anhalt, Thüringen,
Rheinland-Pfalz; Mecklenburg-Vorpommern (Philosophieren mit Kindern),
Schleswig-Holstein (some schools), Niedersachsen (Werte und Normen, since
2022), Hessen (being phased in). NRW teaches Praktische Philosophie from
Klasse 1 for children not in Religion, rising year by year from the
children who started school on 1 August 2022; how far it has reached is
still open. None in Grundschule: Baden-Württemberg, Berlin, Hamburg, Bremen,
Saarland (Brandenburg teaches L-E-R from Klasse 5). That is one more reason
it comes last; the skills overlap with `world.society` and could live there
if Ethik is dropped.

## Structure

Proposed domain `ethics`, four progressions following Bayern, which NRW's
five areas map onto:

| Progression | Content | NRW |
|---|---|---|
| `ethics.self` | feelings, wishes, strengths, trying again | Das Ich |
| `ethics.together` | friendship, fairness, rules, helping, truth, justice | Das Du, Das Wir |
| `ethics.beliefs` | festivals, places and symbols of the world religions, big questions | Sichtweisen auf die Welt |
| `ethics.modern-world` | nature and what people make, animals, media habits | Umwelt und Technik |

About 18 skills, 36 lessons. Dropped from the first draft: `courage` and
`happiness` (neither curriculum asks for them). Overlaps with
`world.society` (`feelings-needs`, `children-rights`, `media-types`): link
them as prerequisites.

Left out for now, pending a decision like the one on sex education: the
origin and limits of life and death (NRW Sichtweisen auf die Welt, Bayern
3/4). It is sensitive for a child alone with an app.

## Fit

Partial: Ethik lives in conversation. Lessons use short stories and
dilemmas with a choice and a reason, never a single right answer where
there is none. Answer checking needs a task type for "pick the reasons that
fit" rather than right and wrong.

## Out of scope

Class discussions, philosophising with others, role play.
