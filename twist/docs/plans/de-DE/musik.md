# Musik

Priority 3. Graded in every Land, no weight in the transfer. Fits the app
well: most of it is listening.

## Official areas

| NRW (Lehrplan Musik) | Bayern (LehrplanPLUS Musik) |
|---|---|
| Musik machen und gestalten | Sprechen – Singen – Musizieren |
| Musik hören und verstehen | Musik – Mensch – Zeit |
| Musik umsetzen und darstellen | Bewegung – Tanz – Szene |
| | Musik und ihre Grundlagen |

Twist covers the app-suitable part: NRW "Musik hören und verstehen" and
Bayern "Musik – Mensch – Zeit" and "Musik und ihre Grundlagen". Making
music together, singing, moving and dancing stay in class. Bayern has a
one-hour and a two-hour version for Klasse 3 and 4; skills found only in
the two-hour version are marked BY2.

## Structure

Proposed domain `music`, shared with en-GB later.

| Progression | Content |
|---|---|
| `music.listening` | high and low, loud and soft, fast and slow, dynamics and tempo changes, form, what music does |
| `music.reading` | graphic notation, then note values, rests, stave, simple rhythms |
| `music.instruments` | instruments by sound, instrument families, works and composers |

## Components

- Drawn stave with notes and rests; a graphic-notation strip for Klasse 1
  and 2 (pages/player/drawn.js).
- New task type: tap the rhythm, timed against a beat (docs/task-types.md).
- Instrument sound bank: real recordings with clear licences, not
  generated.

## Rights

- Saint-Saëns, Karneval der Tiere (published 1922): public domain in the EU
  and the US.
- Prokofiev, Peter und der Wolf (1936): public domain in Germany and the EU
  since 1 January 2024; still protected in the US, probably to the end of
  2031. Fine for de-DE; check before en-GB or a US market uses it.
- Recordings carry their own rights (70 years from publication in the EU),
  and so do German narrator texts. Record our own or license them.

## Out of scope

Singing and playing together, dancing, scenes, performing.
