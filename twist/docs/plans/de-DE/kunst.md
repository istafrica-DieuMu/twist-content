# Kunst (art)

Priority 4. Graded, no weight in the transfer. Most of the subject is making
art with real material; Twist covers seeing, knowing and choosing.

## Official areas

| NRW (Lehrplan Kunst) | Bayern (LehrplanPLUS Kunst) |

## Structure

Proposed domain `art`, three progressions:

| Progression | Content | NRW, Bayern |
|---|---|---|
| `art.colour-form` | colours, mixing, warm and cold, light and dark; lines, shapes, formats, materials | Malen, Zeichnen; Bildende Kunst |
| `art.looking` | describing pictures, techniques, genres, artists, times and cultures, architecture and design | Bilder betrachten; Bildende Kunst, Gestaltete Umwelt |
| `art.media` | photo and film: Motiv, Ausschnitt, perspective | Fotografieren und Filmen; Visuelle Medien |

## Components

- Colour-mixing drawing (two paints in, result out).
- Colour wheel.
- Camera frame for choosing a detail of a picture (Ausschnitt).

## Rights

Use works by artists who died in 1955 or earlier (EU: life plus 70 years),
for example Monet, van Gogh, Marc, Macke, Kandinsky, Klee, Matisse.
Picasso, Miró and Giacometti, whom Bayern names for Klasse 1 and 2, are
still protected; show them only with a licence. Photos of public-domain
paintings are free to use in the EU (Art. 14 DSM Directive), but take them
from sources that state it.

## Out of scope

Painting, drawing, printing, modelling, building, textile work and
performing with real material; exhibitions.
