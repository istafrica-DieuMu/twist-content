## Enrichment

Placed although no KMK standard asks for them, each with its reason in
curricula/de-DE.yaml: written division by a one-digit number and with
remainders and zeros (Bayern still teaches it), Roman numerals. Klasse 4
"Erweiterung" also holds binary numbers, data sizes, divisibility rules and
point symmetry.
