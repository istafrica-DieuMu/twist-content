# Naturwissenschaften (sciences): Biologie (biology), Chemie (chemistry), Physik (physics)

Sekundarstufe I, Klasse 5 to 10. Drafted 2026-10-24 against the KMK
Bildungsstandards for the MSA (2024), the NRW Kernlehrpläne for the G9
Gymnasium (2019) and the Realschule (2011), and Bayern LehrplanPLUS
Gymnasium (Natur und Technik (science and technology) 5 to 7; Biologie, Physik and Chemie (NTG) 8
to 10). Conventions as in README.md in this folder.

## Structure

### Official areas

**KMK (MSA, 2024).** The three subjects share four Kompetenzbereiche:
Sachkompetenz, Erkenntnisgewinnungskompetenz, Kommunikationskompetenz,
Bewertungskompetenz (Biologie text lines 244 to 252). Each subject adds
Basiskonzepte and, new in 2024, a list of "verbindliche inhaltliche
Aspekte":

| Subject | Basiskonzepte | Content areas |
|---|---|---|
| Biologie | Struktur und Funktion; Stoff- und Energieumwandlung; Information und Kommunikation; Steuerung und Regelung; individuelle und evolutive Entwicklung | Lebewesen bestehen aus Zellen; Vielfalt und Veränderung von Lebewesen; Der Mensch als Lebewesen; Lebewesen in ihrer Umwelt |
| Chemie | Aufbau und Eigenschaften der Stoffe und ihrer Teilchen; chemische Reaktion; Energie | Aufbau und Eigenschaften von Stoffen und Teilchen; Chemische Reaktion; Energie |
| Physik | Erhaltung und Gleichgewichte; Modelle und Vorhersagen; Experimente und Verfahren; Ursache und Wirkung | Energie; Elektrizitätslehre; Mechanik; Elektromagnetische Strahlung; Ionisierende Strahlung |

KMK has no Bildungsstandards for the sciences at ESA level (the 2003/2004
ESA standards cover Deutsch (German), Mathe (maths) and the first foreign language only).

**NRW G9 (2019).** Biologie and Physik are taught from Klasse 5 or 6
(Erprobungsstufe), Chemie from Klasse 7. Schools may teach the three
integrated in Klasse 5 and 6 with approval (each KLP, e.g. Biologie lines
320 to 330). Two stages: end of Klasse 6 (Erprobungsstufe), end of Sek I.

| Subject | Erprobungsstufe (5/6) | Stufe 2 (7 to 10) |
|---|---|---|
| Biologie | 1 Vielfalt und Angepasstheiten von Lebewesen; 2 Mensch und Gesundheit; 3 Sexualerziehung | 4 Ökologie und Naturschutz; 5 Evolution; 6 Genetik; 7 Mensch und Gesundheit; 8 Sexualerziehung |
| Physik | 1 Temperatur und Wärme; 2 Elektrischer Strom und Magnetismus; 3 Schall; 4 Licht | 5 Optische Instrumente; 6 Sterne und Weltall; 7 Bewegung, Kraft und Energie; 8 Druck und Auftrieb; 9 Elektrizität; 10 Ionisierende Strahlung und Kernenergie; 11 Energieversorgung |
| Chemie | (erste Stufe) 1 Stoffe und Stoffeigenschaften; 2 Chemische Reaktion; 3 Verbrennung; 4 Metalle und Metallgewinnung | (zweite Stufe) 5 Elemente und ihre Ordnung; 6 Salze und Ionen; 7 Chemische Reaktionen durch Elektronenübertragung; 8 Molekülverbindungen; 9 Saure und alkalische Lösungen; 10 Organische Chemie |

NRW Basiskonzepte differ from KMK: Biologie System, Struktur und
Funktion, Entwicklung; Chemie Struktur der Materie, Chemische Reaktion,
Energie; Physik Energie, Struktur der Materie, Wechselwirkung, System.

### Twist domains

One domain per science, as the Länder and the KMK split them from Klasse 7
at the latest:

| Domain | Progressions | Mirrors |
|---|---|---|
| `biology` | `biology.inquiry` (Arbeitsweisen), `biology.cells` (Zellen und Stoffwechsel), `biology.diversity` (Vielfalt, Vererbung, Evolution), `biology.human` (Der Mensch), `biology.ecology` (Lebewesen in ihrer Umwelt) | KMK content areas, one each; inquiry for Erkenntnisgewinnung |
| `chemistry` | `chemistry.inquiry` (Arbeiten im Labor), `chemistry.substances` (Stoffe und Stoffgruppen), `chemistry.particles` (Teilchen, Atome, Bindungen), `chemistry.reactions` (Reaktionen und Energie) | KMK Basiskonzepte; the energy concept sits in reactions |
| `physics` | `physics.inquiry` (Messen und Modelle), `physics.energy` (Energie und Wärme), `physics.electricity` (Elektrizität und Magnetismus), `physics.mechanics` (Mechanik, Druck, Schall), `physics.light` (Licht, Strahlung, Klima, Himmel), `physics.nuclear` (Ionisierende Strahlung) | KMK content areas; heat joins energy, sound joins mechanics |

**Klasse 5 and 6 combined subjects.** NaWi (combined science), Natur und Technik and BNT do
not get a domain. Their skills are the same `biology` and `physics` skills
(and the few chemistry ideas Bayern teaches propädeutisch in NT 5, which
Twist places in `chemistry` Klasse 7 with a note). de-DE shows Biologie
and Physik as two subjects in Klasse 5 and 6, as NRW G9 does. For a
child at a school with a combined subject, a profile setting could group
both under "Naturwissenschaften" (or "Natur und Technik"); the skills and
lessons stay the same. This needs the curriculum format to allow one
displayed subject over two domains (open question for the build).

### Notes column

As README.md, with one change for the sciences: KMK has no ESA standard,
so **ESA** here means basic content that every school type teaches (it is
in the first stage of the NRW Realschule KLP and in Klasse 5/6 of the
Gymnasium). Confirm against a Hauptschule or Mittelschule curriculum
before the framework records (see Sources). **MSA** means the item is on
the KMK MSA list or clearly derived from it. **G** means only the
Gymnasium curricula ask for it. **one Land (X)** means only one of the
captured curricula asks for it.

## Out of scope for an app

To record in ../omitted.md with these reasons:

- **Hands-on lab work**: Schülerexperimente, Gasbrenner, titration,
  electrolysis set-ups, measuring with ammeters and voltmeters on real
  circuits, Bayern's Profilbereich at the NTG. Twist teaches the plan,
  the prediction and the evaluation of an experiment with real data and
  simulations; the experiment itself stays in class. Safe home
  experiments are offered as Probier's aus (below).
- **Dissection and preparing specimens** (Präparieren, Bayern NT 6;
  common for fish, heart, eye). Replaced by detailed drawings and photos.
- **Microscope practice and field work**: using a microscope, collecting
  and identifying plants outdoors, herbarium, Schulgarten, exploring a
  local ecosystem (NRW IF 4, Bayern 8). Twist teaches the parts and steps
  and offers identification keys with photos.
- **Sex education beyond the biology of puberty**: contraception,
  pregnancy and abortion, sexual orientation, sexually transmitted
  infections, prevention of abuse (NRW IF 3 and 8, Bayern NT 5 and B8 3).
  Follows the 2026-10-24 decision for Grundschule. `puberty-hormones`
  stays as hormone biology; the team decides whether that is the right
  boundary for ages 13 to 14 before authoring.
- **Bayern NTG-only depth in Chemie**: orbital model, mesomerism, E/Z
  isomers, aldehydes and ketones, nucleophile-electrophile mechanisms,
  fats and soaps at molecular level (Bayern C9, C10). One Land, one
  Ausbildungsrichtung, beyond the KMK list. Candidates for later
  enrichment.
- **Informatik inside Natur und Technik** (Bayern NT 6 and 7): belongs to
  weitere.md (Informatik).
- **Group projects and presentations** (Bayern NT 5 "Dokumentieren und
  Präsentieren", NT 6 project): class work.
- **Ethical debates as open discussion** (gene technology, prenatal
  diagnostics, nuclear power). Twist keeps the Bewertungskompetenz as
  choose-and-justify tasks with criteria (fun.md, Ethik (ethics) style), not as an
  open debate.

## Fun at ages 10 to 16

The rules in ../fun.md hold (open with a question, the child does
something, one story, real pictures, one "Wusstest du?", short and
varied, humour). At 10 to 16 the tone changes: no mascot baby talk, more
real data, more choice, more challenge. Hooks:

| Hook | What it is | Used in |
|---|---|---|
| Probier's aus (home lab) | Safe experiments with kitchen material, predict first, report after; never counts for mastery. Cress germination, red-cabbage indicator with vinegar and baking soda, felt-tip chromatography on a coffee filter, growing salt crystals, a yeast balloon, pineapple in jelly (enzymes), lemon battery with a coin and a zinc nail lighting an LED, bottle-and-balloon expansion, rubber-band guitar, pencil in a glass of water, egg in salt water, ruler drop for reaction time, blind spot test, pulse before and after exercise | `seed-germination`, `acids-bases-everyday`, `separation`, `salts`, `fermentation`, `enzyme-factors`, `batteries`, `thermal-expansion`, `pitch-loudness`, `refraction`, `buoyancy`, `reflexes`, `eye`, `heart-circulation` |

| Real data | Their own numbers: the family's electricity bill, the speed of a football shot, the noise level at a concert, a phone charger's efficiency | `electric-power`, `speed`, `noise`, `efficiency` |

## Size and benchmark

258 skills, about 535 lessons. Pictures: about 1500 (animals, plants,
organs, lab equipment, everyday objects), at about $0.02 each under $40;
diagrams are drawn components, not pictures.

The benchmark's courses for these subjects:

| Subject | Benchmark | Twist plan |
|---|---|---|
| Biologie | 119 lessons over Klasse 5 to 10 (35 + 52 + 32); Klasse 7/8 and 9/10 marked as incomplete | 102 skills, about 210 lessons |
| Chemie | 63 lessons over Klasse 7 to 10 (40 + 23); no acids and bases, no organic chemistry, no redox with electrons | 66 skills, about 140 lessons |
| Physik | 19 lessons, Klasse 5 to 8 only | 90 skills, about 185 lessons |
| Science (Klasse 3 to 6 course) | overlaps Sachunterricht, not Sek I | n/a |

Twist plans about 2.7 times the benchmark's lessons, mostly in Physik
(the benchmark stops at Klasse 8 and covers none of energy conservation,
electromagnetism, induction or nuclear physics, all on the KMK MSA list)
and in Chemie Klasse 9 and 10. Biologie is close in scope; Twist adds
ecology depth (cycles, energy flow) and drops some single-animal
portraits (Igel, Maulwurf, Eichhörnchen) into the class-level skills.
