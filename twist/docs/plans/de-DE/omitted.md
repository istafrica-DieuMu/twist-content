# What de-DE deliberately leaves out

Everything Twist decided not to teach for de-DE, with the reason and the
date. A teacher or parent asking "why is X missing?" should find the answer
here. Out-of-scope standards also go into curricula/de-DE.yaml
`out_of_scope` once their framework record exists; that file is the source
for the build, this one is the overview.

Status: **out** (decided), **later** (not now, may come back), **open**
(waiting for a decision).

## Subjects

| What | Status | Reason | Decided |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sport | out | taught with the body, in a gym or on a field | 2026-10-24 |
| Werken, Textiles Gestalten | out | taught with real tools and material | 2026-10-24 |
| Religion | out | split by denomination and different in every Land; Ethik covers values for every family. Applies to Sekundarstufe I too | 2026-10-24 |
| Medienbildung, Informatik as Grundschule subjects | out | the Länder teach them inside Sachunterricht; Twist does the same (`world.society`, `world.technology`). From Klasse 5 Informatik is its own subject (`computing`, sek1/weitere.md) | 2026-10-24 |
| Vorschule (age 5 to 6) | later | not a school year in most Länder; would be the German counterpart of Reception | 2026-10-24 |

### Deutsch

Ten KMK Deutsch standards need other children or a real place (in
curricula/de-DE.yaml since phase 1):

| Standard | Reason |
|---|---|
| sz.7 | feedback to classmates on their talks happens in class |
| sz.15 | taking roles in a group conversation needs other children |
| sz.18 | talking with the class about one's own learning happens in class |
| sz.20 | board, poster and moderation cards are classroom tools |
| sz.22 | scenic speaking and play are rehearsed and performed together |
| sch.16 | cooperative planning of a text needs a writing partner |
| sch.26 | giving and asking for feedback on texts happens between children |
| tm.8 | finding one's way in a library is learnt in a real library |
| tm.21 | the literary conversation is a group discussion |
| tm.30 | staging texts scenically is done together in class |

Free writing is taught but not marked automatically (lesson-anatomy.md).

## Sekundarstufe I (Klasse 5 to 10)

Each subject doc in sek1/ has the full list with sources; this is the
overview. Decided 2026-10-24 unless marked open.

### Across all subjects

| What | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Lab work, experiments, measuring, field work, excursions, microscopes, dissection | out (in class) | need equipment or a real place; Twist teaches plan, prediction and evaluation with real data and simulations, and offers safe home experiments (Probier's aus) |
| Group work, debates, role play, peer feedback, projects, presentations to an audience | out | need other people; Twist teaches the rules and moves, and the child can record and self-check |
| Free writing marked automatically (essays, Erörterung, interpretation, proofs, free translation) | out; automatic feedback open | taught in checkable parts; the full text gets a model and a self-check, never a grade |
| Land-only and school-branch-only content (Bayern history and constitution, NTG and MuG depth, Heimatraum, Profilbereiche) | out | Germany-wide rule (README decision 3) |
| Copyrighted novels, films, songs, recordings, art after 1955 | out unless licensed | Twist writes its own or uses public-domain works |
| Using named software products (office, spreadsheets, GeoGebra, full IDEs, real hardware) | out | product interfaces change; Twist uses small built-in tools (table, calculator, code editor, simulated sensors) |
| Oberstufe topics (calculus, vectors, binomial distribution, logarithm functions) | later | Oberstufe is later |

### Erdkunde, Geschichte, Politik, Wirtschaft

| What | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|

colonial genocide, slavery, Stasi: no humour, no celebration animation, no
photos of victims) are in sek1/gesellschaft.md.
