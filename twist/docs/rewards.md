# Stars and badges

Stars show where a child stands on each skill. Badges mark bigger steps and
stay rare. Both follow the mastery rule, so nothing that does not count for
mastery earns them. Both curricula use the same designs and rules; only the
names are translated, and only the year milestones differ, because the years
differ. Parents see the same stars and badges on the dashboard. The
mastery states are in lesson-anatomy.md (Mastery).

## Stars

Each skill shows 0 to 3 stars, set by its mastery state, never by a score.

| Stars | State |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not started, Learning |
| 1 | Practised |
| 2 | Secure |
| 3 | Mastered |

The 3rd star comes when the skill's spaced recaps are passed, about 1.5
weeks after `Secure` for ages 4 to 5, 2 weeks for ages 6 to 7 and 3 to 4
weeks for ages 8 to 10 (lesson-anatomy.md, Recaps). Most recaps arrive as
the warm-up of a later lesson, so the mascot simply brings an old skill back
("Weißt du das noch?" / "Do you still know this?"). Until then the 3rd star
shows empty and fills a little each day; from age 8 it also says when the
skill comes back.

`Needs a recap` keeps the stars and adds a refresh mark. Stars are never
taken away. Practise tasks inside lessons and practice sessions earn no
stars; the end of a practice session shows what was done ("3 recaps done").

## Rules for every badge

- A badge is never taken away.
- A badge earned again in a later year levels up and carries that year:
  "Leseratte Klasse 3", "Bookworm Year 3". One set covers Reception to
  Year 6 and every foreign-language learning year.
- When every skill behind a badge is `Mastered`, the badge turns gold. Gold
  is a new look on the same badge, not a new badge.
- German names are things, animals or words that fit every child, so no
  badge needs a male and a female form.
- Speed, perfect scores, quick checks and Practise tasks earn nothing.

A school day is Monday to Friday, without the public and school holidays of
the child's region (the Bundesland, or England). It counts when the child
finishes at least one lesson, practice session or check that day. Practice
at the weekend is welcome but never needed. A missed school day ends the
current streak; the best streak stays on show.

## Strand badges

One per strand and year, earned when every skill of that strand placed in
the child's year is `Secure`. Strands group the library areas, so both
curricula share them. Topics are too small for badges (de-DE has 161, en-GB
241, and en-GB Maths averages under 2 skills a topic).

| Subject | Strand | Library areas | EN | DE |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Milestones

| EN | DE | Earned when |
|---|---|---|
| Lift-off | Startschuss | The first skill becomes `Secure` |
| Pathfinder | Startklar | The first placement check is finished |
| Summit | Gipfel | The subject's end-of-year check is passed ("Mathe-Gipfel Klasse 3"); one per subject and year |
| Hat Trick | Hattrick | A strand badge in every subject in the same year; one per year |
| Legend | Legende | Every skill of a subject is `Secure` across the whole curriculum (Klasse 4, Year 6, or the last foreign-language learning year) |

## Streaks

School days in a row; each step is its own badge.

| Days | EN | DE |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Spark | Funke |
| 10 | Flame | Flamme |
| 20 | Campfire | Lagerfeuer |
| 40 | Torch | Fackel |
| 60 | Comet | Komet |
| 100 | Volcano | Vulkan |
| 190 | Sun | Sonne |
| 380 | Supernova | Supernova |

190 school days is about one school year in both countries.

## Effort and curiosity

| EN | DE | Levels |
|---|---|---|
| Busy Bee | Fleißiges Bienchen | 10, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000 practice sessions finished |
| Time Traveller | Zeitreise | 1, 10, 25 skills from a later year made `Secure` |

A child who uses all three subjects through a school year earns about 20 to
25 badges: up to 14 strand badges, a few milestones, and some streak and
level steps. The levels and day counts are starting values, tuned from data
like the other thresholds.

## Look

The art is in assets/badges (earned and gold, SVG), drawn by
assets/tools/badges.mjs; frames and colours per group are in
assets/README.md (Badges). Each group has its own frame, so a child can
tell a streak from a strand badge before reading the name.
