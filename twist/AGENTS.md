# Rules for twist/

The root AGENTS.md applies. On top of it:

- **Only finished Twist content lives here.** No raw captures, no source
  ids, no links into source folders. Records do not cite Anton.
- **Anton's text is reference material.** Read it while writing
  competencies, titles and questions. Where our wording ends up the same as
  Anton's, that is fine. Scenarios, examples, names, stories and contexts
  are written for Twist. No Anton images or audio.
- **Official documents** are quoted only in frameworks/, verbatim, with a
  page reference; the documents themselves live in their source folder.
- **Ids never contain a grade or year.** A step or lesson can be placed in
  different stages by different profiles. Ids are lowercase ASCII, umlauts
  written out (ä to ae), and never change once published.
- **Grade, proficiency and age stay separate fields.** Placement (in
  profiles/) says what a grade expects; a learner's step on a progression
  says where they work; a lesson's age band says how it looks.
- **Records are the source of truth.** views/ is generated by build.mjs;
  change the records or the script, never the views.
- **Every competency step maps to the framework objectives that ask for
  it**, or is marked `enrichment` with a reason.
- **Never write into schoolido-learning.** Today's Twist modules are
  referenced by module id in a lesson's `twist_reuse` field.
