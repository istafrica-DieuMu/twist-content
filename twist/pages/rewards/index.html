Stars and badges

Stars show where a child stands on each skill. Badges mark bigger steps and stay rare. Both follow the mastery rule, so nothing that does not count for mastery earns them. Both curricula use the same designs and rules; only the names are translated, and only the year milestones differ, because the years differ. Parents see the same stars and badges on the dashboard. The mastery states are in lesson-anatomy.md (Mastery).

Stars

Each skill shows 0 to 3 stars, set by its mastery state, never by a score.

Not started, Learning
★★★

★★★
Secure
★★★

Needs a recap keeps the stars and adds a refresh mark. Stars are never taken away. Practise tasks inside lessons and practice sessions earn no stars; the end of a practice session shows what was done ("3 recaps done").

Rules for every badge

  • A badge is never taken away.
  • A badge earned again in a later year levels up and carries that year: "Leseratte Klasse 3", "Bookworm Year 3". One set covers Reception to Year 6 and every foreign-language learning year.
  • When every skill behind a badge is Mastered, the badge turns gold. Gold is a new look on the same badge, not a new badge.
  • German names are things, animals or words that fit every child, so no badge needs a male and a female form.
  • Speed, perfect scores, quick checks and Practise tasks earn nothing.

A school day is Monday to Friday, without the public and school holidays of the child's region (the Bundesland, or England). It counts when the child finishes at least one lesson, practice session or check that day. Practice at the weekend is welcome but never needed. A missed school day ends the current streak; the best streak stays on show.

Strand badges

One per strand and year, earned when every skill of that strand placed in the child's year is Secure. Strands group the library areas, so both curricula share them. Topics are too small for badges (de-DE has 161, en-GB 241, and en-GB Maths averages under 2 skills a topic).

A child who uses all three subjects through a school year earns about 20 to 25 badges: up to 14 strand badges, a few milestones, and some streak and level steps. The levels and day counts are starting values, tuned from data like the other thresholds.
