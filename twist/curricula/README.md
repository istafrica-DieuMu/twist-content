# curricula

One file per curriculum: de-DE and en-GB for now. A curriculum lists the frameworks that apply, the standards that are out of scope for an app (each with a reason), and places every skill in a year, in teaching order. Year views are generated from these. A curriculum can also have tracks, subjects of their own with their own placement by learning year (DaZ in de-DE); see docs/model.md, Tracks.
