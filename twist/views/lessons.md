## Farben auf Englisch (Colours in English)

`de-DE.fl.en-colours` teaches `fl.themes.colours` in de-DE, stufe-0. Ages 6-7, about 9 minutes a lesson. Status: draft, version 2.

Eight colours, heard and said before they are ever read. Stufe 0 is oral only: every task can be done without reading. Instructions are spoken in German, the colour words in English. Lesson 1 brings red, blue, yellow and green; lesson 2 adds orange, purple, pink and brown and ends with the skill check. Practice runs in three stages: full hints, one hint, no hints, and the last stage puts the colours on things. Each lesson also pairs colours with things that have them (matching) and sorts pictures by colour (sorting); tapping a colour says its English name.

## Die Buchstaben L, O, A und M (The letters L, O, A and M)

`de-DE.lit-de.letters-l-o-a-m` teaches `lit-de.letters.l-o-a-m` in de-DE, klasse-1. Ages 6-7, about 10 minutes a lesson. Status: draft, version 2.

The first four letters in Fibel order, chosen because their sounds can be held (lll, ooo, aaa, mmm) and they make words at once: Oma, Mama, Lama. One lesson per letter: hear the sound, find it at the start of words, see and trace the letter, practised in three stages (full hints, one hint, no hints). The fifth lesson blends the first syllables, reads three words and ends with the skill check.

## Plusaufgaben mit Zehnerübergang (Adding across ten)

`de-DE.math.add-bridge-10` teaches `math.add-sub-to-20.add-bridge-10` in de-DE, klasse-1. Ages 6-7, about 12 minutes a lesson. Status: draft, version 2.

Children add a one-digit number that crosses ten by filling up to ten first and adding the rest (8 + 5 = 8 + 2 + 3). Lesson 1 shows it on the Zwanzigerfeld, lesson 2 writes the split down and shows it on the Rechenstrich, lesson 3 uses it in short stories and ends with the skill check. Every lesson practises in three stages: full hints, one hint, no hints.
