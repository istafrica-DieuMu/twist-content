Year 3 counts from 0 in multiples of 4, 8, 50 and 100, and in tens from any number across a hundred. Lesson 1 counts in 4s, 8s, 50s and 100s; lesson 2 finds the rule of a sequence, the missing term and the term that breaks the rule, and ends with the skill check.

## Anzahlen auf einen Blick (Seeing amounts at a glance)

`en-GB.math.subitize-to-10` teaches `math.numbers-to-20.subitize-to-10` in en-GB, reception. Ages 4-5, about 8 minutes a lesson. Status: draft, version 1.

Children see how many without counting one by one (subitising). Lesson 1 uses dice patterns to 5, shown for two seconds, as the EYFS goal asks. Lesson 2 goes on to "five and a bit": a full row of a ten frame is five, and seven is five and two. It ends with the skill check.

## Die Ziffern 0 bis 10 (Numerals 0 to 10)

`en-GB.math.write-numerals-to-10` teaches `math.numbers-to-20.write-numerals-to-10` in en-GB, year-1. Ages 6-7, about 11 minutes a lesson. Status: draft, version 1.

Year 1 opens by reading and writing the numerals and matching each to its amount, as children move from Reception's spoken numbers to written ones. Lesson 1 takes 1 to 5, lesson 2 takes 6 to 10, each with how the numeral is formed; lesson 3 adds 0 and the number words one to ten (the National Curriculum asks for numerals and words in Year 1) and ends with the skill check. When a task asks which numeral fits, the numeral options have no speaker, because hearing "three" would give the answer.
