# German: how Anton sequences it, and what that means for Twist

Phase 3 item 8, 2026-10-09. Input for the German en-LOI repair in
schoolido-learning. Sources: the course structure in
[catalogue/catalogue.db](catalogue/), the crosswalks
[anton-en-german.md](crosswalk/anton-en-german.md) (Twist German for English
speakers against Anton DaZ) and [anton-de-german.md](crosswalk/anton-de-german.md)
(Twist German for German speakers against Anton Deutsch), and
[calibration.md](calibration.md). All matching rests on lesson titles, not
exercise content; the crosswalks say where that makes a match uncertain.

## Anton keeps two German subjects apart

- **Deutsch** is German for native speakers, by Klasse.
- **DaZ (Deutsch als Zweitsprache)** is German as a second language, not
  graded: Alphabetisierung (47 lessons), Kurs A1 (87), Kurs A2 (39),
  Vokabel-Training (17 and 40). English-GB also has c-mono-deu-1, German for
  English speakers (40 lessons, the same themes).

Anton never mixes the two in one course.

## How Deutsch builds, Klasse 1 to 4

| Klasse | Strands, in Anton's order (lessons) |
|---|---|
| 1 | Letters and their sounds in Fibel order with reviews (47), syllable reading (3), first words, sentences and texts to read (14), first words and sentences to write (13), speaking and listening (10), Blitzlesen (4) |
| 2 | Grammatik (19), Rechtschreibung (31, of which Rechtschreibstrategien 11), Lesen (13), Schreiben (8), Blitzlesen (4), Schreibschrift in three scripts (60) |
| 3 | Grammatik (35, new: Satzglieder 5, Wörtliche Rede 3), Rechtschreibung (33, strategies 12, dictionary, error correction), Lesen (17), Schreiben: plan, write, revise (11), Blitzlesen (4) |
| 4 | Grammatik (28, Satzglieder 6, Sprache erforschen 3), Rechtschreibung (21, strategies review 4), Lesen (13, incl. presenting), Schreiben (5) |

The shape: decoding first (letter by letter, then syllables, then words,
sentences, texts), then from Klasse 2 four parallel strands (grammar,
spelling, reading, writing) that return every year at a higher level.
Spelling strategies (Silbenschwingen, Verlängern, Ableiten, Merkwörter) are
the backbone of Klasse 2 and 3. Handwriting is its own block in Klasse 2.
Sentences in item text grow from a median of 6 words (Klasse 1) to 8
(Klasse 3 and 4).

## How DaZ builds

Alphabetisierung teaches letters and sounds (47 lessons). Kurs A1 is
thematic: welcome, school, classroom phrases, hobbies, shopping, food,
countries, people and feelings, health, animals, home, seasons, weather,
time. Grammar rides on the themes: Anton's A1 and A2 already reach Perfekt,
stem-changing verbs, plurals, the accusative and adjective endings.

## Where Twist stands

**Twist German for English speakers (en LOI)** changes track inside G2:

| Twist grade | Track | Evidence (anton-en-german.md) |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | foreign language | matches DaZ and c-mono-deu-1 themes (Farben, Zahlen, Körper, Familie, Schule, Tiere) |
| G2 | mostly foreign language | 10 native-style literacy lessons (word families, compounds, affixes, paragraphs, creative writing, poetry) have no DaZ match |
| G3 | native speaker | 17 of 21 matched lessons match Deutsch Klasse 3/4, against 10 for DaZ |
| G4 | native speaker | 16 of 17 against 6 |

So an English-speaking child moves from German as a foreign language to
native-speaker German between G2 and G3, with no bridge. Anton never asks
that of a learner: DaZ A1 then A2 is its path for the same child.

**Twist German for German speakers (de LOI)**: 70 of 103 lessons match Anton
Deutsch, almost all partially; 19 of the 33 unmatched lessons are DaZ-style
vocabulary or culture content in a native-speaker course
(anton-de-german.md).

## Gaps against Anton, by track

- **en LOI, against DaZ:** shopping (with numbers to 100 and money),
  clothing and appearance, health, seasons, classroom phrases, and A2 modal
  verbs and comparatives.
- **de LOI, against Deutsch:** systematic letter-by-letter decoding with
  reviews, syllable reading and syllable marking (Anton's `syllabifier`),
  Rechtschreibstrategien as a strand, handwriting (Schreibschrift), and
  Blitzlesen (timed reading).
- **Both:** Twist teaches some grammar later than Anton's DaZ (Perfekt,
  plurals, adjective endings at A1/A2 against Twist G3/G4), and some literacy
  earlier than Anton's Deutsch (compounds, affixes, poetry in Twist G2,
  Anton Klasse 3).

## For the repair

Options, not decisions:

1. **One track per LOI.** en LOI German follows a foreign-language path all
   the way (A1 themes for G1/G2, A2 themes and grammar for G3/G4), and de LOI
   German follows a native-speaker path (decoding, strands, strategies). This
   removes the G2/G3 switch.
2. **If G3/G4 en LOI stays native-style**, add a bridge in G2: the grammar
   Anton's DaZ teaches at A1/A2 (Perfekt, plurals, accusative) before the
   native-speaker texts arrive.
3. **For de LOI German**, the four Anton gaps (syllables, spelling
   strategies, handwriting, timed reading) are what German primary teachers
   and parents check against; they map to item types Twist lacks today
   (type-gaps.md, ranks 5 and 6) or has (`tracing` for handwriting).

One inconsistency in Twist's own catalogue was flagged by the en German
crosswalk and not checked here: G2 defers "weil" to G4, while G3 Reading:
sequencing lists weil/deshalb.
