# Lesson mechanics

Step 1e note, 2026-10-09, from the course files (1b) and the browser pass
(1d, `notes/1d-log.md`). Terms are defined in
[../../taxonomy.md](../../taxonomy.md#lesson-mechanics).

## Structure

- **Course → topic → lesson → exercise → item.** In Anton's files: plan →
  topic → block → level → atom.
- **Lessons are short.** Median 6 exercises per lesson; 90% of the 5,213
  lessons have 4 to 8; the range is 2 to 48.
- **Exercises are about 14 items** (1f files so far). The first exercise
  played had 6 items.
- **Tests are their own exercises.** 4,564 of 31,804 exercises are marked
  `test`, roughly one per lesson. 251 are kind `bulb` (meaning not
  confirmed).
- **Two lesson layouts.** A vertical path of tiles (`design: map`) or a list
  of named exercises (`02-lesson-zahlen-vergleichen.png`,
  `51-lesson-einzahl-mehrzahl.png`).

## Inside an exercise

- **Instruction read aloud.** Every instruction has a speaker icon; the top
  bar replays it. Some exercises need sound and say so.
- **Feedback per answer, retry in place.** Right: green frame or tick and a
  green progress segment. Wrong: red frame and a red segment; the item stays
  and the learner tries again (`11-memory-wrong-feedback.png`,
  `53-dragMatch-wrong.png`).
- **Reveal.** After a mistake a **Lösen** button can show the answer;
  revealed items show as grey ticks and a grey segment, counted neither right
  nor wrong (`24-findAll-reveal.png`).
- **Check on request** for constructive tasks: **Prüfen** in dragAndValidate,
  mathGrid, dragSort and shapeMirror.
- **Hints** exist as a **Tipp** button with a bulb on some exercises
  (`c0-contentPlayer-start.png`), not on every item.
- **Tests behave like practice per item**: a wrong answer gets the same red
  segment and a retry (`41-test-wrong.png`).
- **Quitting asks** "Jetzt abbrechen?" (`25-after-cancel.png`).

## Rewards and progression

- **Stars:** up to three per exercise, shown on the lesson path. Three stars
  after one wrong tap in 24 (`13-exercise-end.png`).
- **Trophies** instead of stars for tests (`51-lesson-einzahl-mehrzahl.png`).
- **Coins:** 2 after one finished exercise; spent on games (Hidden, Astro
  Bang, Block Puzzle, ...) on the Spiele page (`m2-spiele.png`).
- **End screen:** Anton character, a short cheer ("Geschafft! Sauber
  abgeliefert."), stars, retry, Weiter, Meine Ergebnisse.
- **No locks in school courses**; every exercise can be opened directly.
  **Preschool puzzle courses do lock**: tiles open in sequence
  (`l0-kidColorize-start-course.png`).
