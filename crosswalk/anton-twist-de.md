## Twist steps with no Anton lesson

What the curriculum adds to Anton, mostly from the KMK standards.

### Mathe (42)

- Kl. 1: `math.numbers-to-20.subitize-to-10` I can see amounts up to 10 at a glance, on dice, fingers or a ten-frame, using groups of 5.
- Kl. 1: `math.word-problems.picture-stories` I can find the calculation for a picture or a short story, solve it and say the answer.
- Kl. 1: `math.add-sub-to-20.equations` I can compare calculations with <, > and = and show that different calculations can have the same result (7 + 5 = 6 + 6).
- Kl. 1: `math.time.sequence-of-day` I can put the events of a day in order and name the days of the week and the months in order.
- Kl. 1: `math.shapes.compose` I can lay, copy, split and build shapes from pieces (for example with tangrams).
- Kl. 1: `math.data.sort-classify` I can sort things by a property and count how many are in each group.
- Kl. 2: `math.add-sub-to-100.describe-methods` I can compare different methods, explain mine, and say which method suits which calculation.
- Kl. 2: `math.word-problems.sketches` I can use sketches, tables and empty number lines to understand and solve a word problem.
- Kl. 2: `math.word-problems.write-stories` I can make up a story that fits a calculation or a picture.
- Kl. 2: `math.patterns.number-patterns` I can continue patterns of numbers and calculations, explain the rule and find the odd one out.
- Kl. 2: `math.money.euro-cent` I can convert between pounds and pence (£1 = 100p) and write amounts in both.
- Kl. 2: `math.time.five-minutes` I can read and set the time to five minutes and know there are 60 minutes in an hour and 24 hours in a day.
- Kl. 2: `math.length.compare` I can compare and order lengths directly (longer, shorter, the same).
- Kl. 2: `math.mass-capacity.compare-capacity` I can compare which container holds more and say whether it is full, half full or empty.
- Kl. 2: `math.length.non-standard` I can measure lengths with hand spans, steps or objects and explain why we need standard units.
- Kl. 2: `math.data.read-tables` I can read information from tables and calendars and use it to answer questions.
- Kl. 2: `math.chance.combinations` I can work out systematically how many possibilities there are (for example outfits), using a table or tree diagram.
- Kl. 2: `math.chance.certain-possible-impossible` I can describe events as certain, likely, unlikely or impossible.
- Kl. 3: `math.mult-div.vocabulary` I can use the words times, divided by, product and quotient correctly.
- Kl. 3: `math.written-add-sub.add-several` I can add more than two numbers in columns, also with different numbers of digits.
- Kl. 3: `math.patterns.functional-tables` I can show relationships such as quantity and price in tables and fill in missing values.
- Kl. 3: `math.money.decimal-notation` I can read and write money amounts with a decimal point (£3.50 = £3 and 50p = 350p).
- Kl. 3: `math.fractions.halves-quarters` I can split a whole into halves and quarters and check whether the parts are equal.
- Kl. 3: `math.space.plans-maps` I can find my way with plans and maps, draw routes and find squares with grid references like A3.
- Kl. 3: `math.data.plan-survey` I can plan a simple survey, organise the data in a table and present the result.
- Kl. 4: `math.written-add-sub.choose-method` I can decide whether to calculate mentally, informally or in columns, and give a reason.
- Kl. 4: `math.mult-div-larger.large-mental` I can multiply and divide mentally with multiples of 10, 100 and 1000 (40 × 300, 36 000 ÷ 9).
- Kl. 4: `math.word-problems.estimate-or-exact` I can decide whether an estimate is enough in a situation or an exact answer is needed.
- Kl. 4: `math.word-problems.check-plausible` I can check whether my answer fits the situation and improve my solution if it does not.
- Kl. 4: `math.patterns.proportional` I can solve problems with proportional relationships by working from what I know to one and then to the amount I need.
- Kl. 4: `math.patterns.codes` I can crack and invent codes that use numbers.
- Kl. 4: `math.mass-capacity.data-units` I can compare data sizes in bytes, kilobytes and megabytes and judge what takes a lot of storage.
- Kl. 4: `math.fractions.everyday-measures` I can understand everyday fractions of measures and convert them to smaller units (½ m = 50 cm, ¾ hour = 45 minutes, ¼ l = 250 ml).
- Kl. 4: `math.shapes.circles-compass` I can draw circles and arcs with compasses and name the centre and radius.
- Kl. 4: `math.shapes.grid-drawing` I can copy and draw shapes and buildings on square and dot grids.
- Kl. 4: `math.shapes.tessellations` I can continue, describe and invent frieze patterns and tessellations.
- Kl. 4: `math.space.mental-moves` I can imagine how a shape or a dice looks after it is turned or tipped over.
- Kl. 4: `math.symmetry.in-environment` I can spot and describe symmetry and reflections around me, in patterns and in art.
- Kl. 4: `math.area-volume.volume-cubes` I can find and compare the volume of cube buildings and cuboids by counting unit cubes.
- Kl. 4: `math.data.critical` I can spot when a chart is misleading, for example because its axis does not start at zero, and read it correctly.
- Kl. 4: `math.data.digital-tools` I can enter data in a table with a digital tool and turn it into a chart.
- Kl. 4: `math.large-numbers.binary` I can convert small numbers between base ten and binary.
